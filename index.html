TTT × PC-ALM · Inference-Time Learning

Independent research · Ongoing

Studying what a pretrained base needs for inference-time self-iteration: six requirements spanning representation, uncertainty, and local credit, with TTT × PC-ALM as a constructive instance.

  • Constructed a mechanism witness in which local multipliers alter cross-branch dynamics, with controlled comparisons against strong regression and matched-parameter optimizers.
  • The public snapshot preserves negative results, resource accounting, and a frozen confirmation protocol; an independent average advantage on new tasks remains unconfirmed.

Ongoing

Native Sparse-Attention Pretraining

Sep. 2026 — Present · Independent research

Built a full-parameter pretraining and evaluation pipeline around a roughly 95M-parameter BabyLM GDN + Attention model, comparing dense attention, learned sparse routing, and fixed local attention.

  • Corrected routing-score scaling: development PPL fell from 53.69 to 51.78 in a single-seed comparison; completed paired local-attention comparisons across two seeds.
  • Audited outputs, losses, and gradients for fast paths and tiled local attention. Some numerical thresholds remain unmet; end-to-end GPU acceleration is unverified.
